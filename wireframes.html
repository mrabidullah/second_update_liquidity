Client · One company

Primary workspace
NAVIGATION | Dashboard, trade history, peer comparison, reports, statements, contract info

Dashboard -> trade history and peer comparison -> reports, statements, company details and contract info.

Multi-company client

Company directory
NAVIGATION | Companies and company-scoped pages
COMPANIES  | Assigned-company count, combined volume, latest data update
TABLE      | Volume, average spread, data freshness, subscription status
ACTION     | Open a company to view its workspace

The Companies page has a combined overview and company table; opening a company leads to its workspace.

Guest

Invited access
INVITATION | Accept invitation to a named company
JOIN FORM  | Name, invited email, password, confirmation
ACCESS     | Read-only, scoped company access
ACTION     | Accept invitation
DESTINATION| Overview, reports and trade history

Guest scope is limited to the company and pages assigned by an administrator.

Administrator

Operations
ADMIN PAGES   | User management, subscriptions, data health, analytics
USERS         | Admin/client lists and company access assignments
SUBSCRIPTIONS  | Plans, start dates, renewal dates and status
DATA HEALTH    | Feed freshness, received records and update time
ANALYTICS     | User access log by date, name, email and company

The project includes company assignments, subscription management, feed health and a user access log. Analytics shows sign-in activity, not an audit of all changes.
